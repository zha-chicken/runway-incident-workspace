# AeroContext promo video

`AeroContext-promo-1080p60.mp4` is a 1 min 50 s promo and demo for 航衡 · AeroContext (HackSHSID 2026, Track 3). It is 1920×1080 at 60 fps, H.264 video with AAC stereo audio, and has bilingual on-screen captions. `AeroContext-promo-poster.png` is a title frame for use as a thumbnail.

## What it shows

The video follows the app's workflow: Incidents → Situation & evidence → Assess & decide → Messages → Ask AeroContext → Feedback, reassessment & export. It ends with the Chinese interface, dark mode and the phone layout.

"Assess & decide" shows a new incident analysed live, followed by its "Why this assessment" breakdown. It then moves to the decision flow on an incident whose reports conflict.

Every app screen in the video is a real capture of commit `8ad14e3`. The cursor, camera moves, 3D tilt and captions were added afterwards.

## The live run

- **The incident.** A new incident, QING "Gusts and expanding support shortage", was created on the running app at port 3001 through its New incident form. It reuses the D02 / UNSEEN02 demo reports with plainer wording.
- **The call.** One real assessment ran on 24 Oct 2026. It took 87 s and returned high risk with four recommendations. It added about 0.17 to the app's development budget (3.33 → 3.50 of 40).
- **How it appears.** The run is shown as a 30× time-lapse, filmed one frame per second while it ran, and is labelled "Live run" in the video.
- **Where it lives.** The recording browser had no session for your workspace, so the app created a fresh workspace for it. That workspace holds the five dataset incidents plus QING. Your existing workspaces, incidents and chats were not changed.

## The other footage

- **Separate copy.** The other chapters were recorded from a separate copy of the app on port 3002, running against a backup of the workspace database.
- **Demo entries.** The two YUN decisions, and the outcome text that begins "Simulated:", were entered in that copy only.
- **Earlier AI output.** The assessment, Ask AeroContext answer and drafts shown for the other incidents are outputs recorded by the app in earlier runs.
- **Labels kept.** Simulation, draft and AI-generated labels stay visible as the app shows them. A footnote in the video says the footage is from the local prototype and the data is simulated.
- **Synthesized audio.** The music and interface sounds were generated by `source/music.py`. They contain no third-party audio.

## Rebuilding

`source/` holds the scripts used to build the video:

1. `capture.mjs` records the screens from the copy server.
2. `live.mjs` creates the QING incident, runs its assessment and films it.
3. `promo.html` and `timeline.js` are the animated composition.
4. `render.mjs` renders the composition frame by frame.
5. `music.py` builds the soundtrack from the timeline's cue sheet.

The captured screenshots themselves are not included, so they have to be recaptured before re-rendering.
